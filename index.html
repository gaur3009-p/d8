Meet the Doctor

Dr. B N Gupta —
Senior Dermatologist

Dr. B N Gupta is one of Lucknow's most respected and experienced dermatologists, with over three decades of uninterrupted specialist practice. As the founder and lead consultant of Dr. Gupta's Skin and Hair Hospital in Hazratganj, he has built a legacy of clinical excellence, diagnostic precision, and compassionate patient care.

Holding an MBBS degree and a post-graduate MD in Dermatology, Dr. Gupta brings depth of knowledge across all areas of skin and hair medicine — from managing complex chronic conditions like psoriasis and vitiligo to performing advanced cosmetic and laser procedures.
